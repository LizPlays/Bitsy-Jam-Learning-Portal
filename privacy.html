What this site knows about you

← Hub

Short answer: almost nothing. This page says exactly what, in plain words, because a site that collects nothing should be able to show its working.

The short version

No accounts. No cookies. No analytics. No tracking. Nothing you click here is sent to anyone who runs this site.

There is no form anywhere on this site. There is nothing here to fill in.

What is kept on your device

One thing. When you press the language button, your browser remembers which language you chose, under the name bj-lang. It holds either en or da and nothing else. It never leaves your browser.

To get rid of it: press the language button again, or clear this site's data in your browser settings. Nothing on the site breaks if you do.

What leaves your device

Three things, none of them chosen by you, so they are listed here rather than buried.

  • The typeface. Every page loads the Nunito font from Google's font service. That request tells Google your IP address and roughly which browser you are using. It happens as the page opens, before you click anything, and Google is outside the EU. The site still reads fine without it — your device falls back to its own font.
  • The host. The site is served by GitHub Pages, and GitHub records visitors' IP addresses in its own server logs. That is GitHub's processing, not ours, and it cannot be turned off on a site hosted this way.
  • Links out. The Play page links to games on itch.io. Nothing is sent to itch.io until you click one, and once you do you are on their site under their rules, not ours.

Pupil work

Nothing published yet

No pupil work is on this site. The Co-discovery Spots are empty because no session has run. These are the rules for when they are not, and they are written down now so they cannot quietly become something else later.

  • Names stay off. Work goes up under a handle you pick, not your name.
  • Nothing goes up by default. Making something does not publish it. It appears only if it is deliberately added, and that step is where consent is given.
  • Thirteen is the age. Under Danish law you can agree to this yourself from thirteen. Below that, a parent decides.
  • Anything comes down on request. You do not have to give a reason, and you do not have to have been the one who put it up.

One thing worth knowing: the site is a public repository, so anything published here can be read by anyone, and its edit history is public too. That is why the rules above are strict.

Who is responsible, and how to ask

Being settled

Who formally answers for this site — the school or the teacher personally — is still being settled, and a contact address goes here once there is one. Saying so is better than putting a name here that turns out to be the wrong one.

Until then, ask in class, or have a parent ask. Nothing will be refused for having been asked the wrong way.
