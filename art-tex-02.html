Textures in 8×8

← Art unit · art-tex-02

Both are four copies of one tile. A straight line has to land exactly on the edge or your eye finds the join before it finds the surface. Scattered marks forgive you.

The constraint

Eight by eight squares. Two colours. Filled or empty, nothing between.

The self-check

Copy your tile four times and tape it into a square. If your eye lands on the join before it lands on the surface, it is not finished.

On your own: hold the taped square at arm’s length instead of asking someone.
